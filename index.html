Q1 / 8TRUST

"If I asked where you stand, where you're headed, and what it'll cost to close the gap — would your data give me one answer, or three?"

One number. Three questions answered.

Where you are. Where you're headed. What it costs to close the gap. If your KPIs can't answer all three without a caveat, they're not metrics yet, they're opinions with a chart attached.

Q2 / 8TEAM

"You've hired the talent. Who's pointing all of it at the same target?"

Talent isn't the gap. Direction is.

Skilled analysts, scientists and engineers pointed at different priorities cost more than an empty seat. A led team compounds. An unled one just gets busier.

Q3 / 8SELF-SERVICE

"If you needed an answer from your data right now, could you get it yourself — or would you have to ask first?"

Confidence shouldn't need a ticket number.

If every answer starts with "let me get back to you", that's not self-service. It's a queue with a friendlier name.

Q4 / 8TRUST GAP

"When your data team hands you their next recommendation — are you agreeing because you understand it, or because you have no way to check it?"

If you can only nod along, that's not a briefing. That's a black box.

A real data trust gap doesn't close with another dashboard. It closes with definitions the leader in the room understands as well as the analyst who built them.

Q5 / 8FOUNDATIONS

"Would your data stack survive the business doubling in size tomorrow?"

Future-proof isn't a word on a vendor's slide.

It's a warehouse that still holds up after the business triples in size. Modernisation that breaks under its own growth wasn't modernisation — it was a rebuild with better branding.

→ Exhibit 04
Q6 / 8OPERATING MODEL

"Or is the newest tool in your stack still running on a five-year-old way of working?"

New platform, old habits, same bottleneck.

Swapping tools without changing how decisions get made and owned doesn't remove the bottleneck. It just moves it to a more expensive address.

→ Exhibit 04→ Exhibit 08
Q7 / 8APPLIED AI

"Could you point to one decision your AI investment has actually changed?"

Not return on investment. Return on intelligence.

AI that doesn't change a decision is a demo, not a result. The right question was never "do we have AI" — it's what did it actually make faster, safer, or cheaper.

→ Exhibit 05
Q8 / 8COMMERCIAL

"If your analyst walked into the boardroom tomorrow, could they make the business case themselves, or would they need you to translate it first?"

A dashboard is not a decision. A business case is.

$11M+ in investment decisions got approved because the analysis was translated into a case leadership could act on, not a chart they had to interpret themselves.
